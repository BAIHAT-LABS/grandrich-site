GrandRich Master

Idle Mining Business Tycoon

GrandRich Master is a tycoon game where you grow a tiny mining claim into a business empire that spans 15 cities. Build mines and factories, trade on the market, spin the fortune machine and climb all the way to GrandRich Master.

Support Privacy Policy

Gameplay

Scan land for ore, run mines and factories, sell at the right market price and raise 15 cities star by star. Rival tycoons are computer-controlled. Progress and settings are stored on your device and no account is needed.

In-App Purchases

Cash packs, spins, chests, ore crates, scans and boosts are sold as In-App Purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card details. Chest drop odds are shown in the game before you buy.

For adults

GrandRich Master is intended for an adult audience. The fortune machine uses in-game currency only. The game does not offer real money gambling or any opportunity to win real money or prizes.
