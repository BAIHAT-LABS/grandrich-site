Privacy Policy — GrandRich Master

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GrandRich Master — Idle Mining Business Tycoon (“GrandRich Master” or “the App”) handles information. By using GrandRich Master, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who we are

GrandRich Master is a mobile tycoon game published by BAIHAT LABS. For privacy requests about GrandRich Master, contact GrandRichMaster@baihatlabs.com.

2. Information we collect

GrandRich Master has no accounts and does not send your game data to our servers. The information involved is:

  • Game progress and settings. Cities, mines, cash and other balances, cards, pets, language, sound and other preferences for GrandRich Master are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Purchase information. When you buy In-App Purchases in GrandRich Master, Apple or Google processes payment and provides the App with enough information to deliver the purchased item (for example, product identifier and transaction status). We do not receive your name, email address or payment card number.
  • Advertising data. GrandRich Master shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus. To deliver, measure and limit the frequency of these ads, Google may collect device information, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic data. On iOS, the device advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you tap Allow in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise ads are shown without it. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form asks for your choice before personalized ads are used. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses data from partner apps).
  • Referral codes. The App creates a random invite code on your device. It is shared only when you choose to send your invite link through an app of your choice, and it contains no personal information.
  • Support communications. If you email us about GrandRich Master, we receive the content of your message and any details you include (such as device type, app version or screenshots) so we can respond.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Provide GrandRich Master gameplay and keep your saved progress on your device;
  • Process and deliver In-App Purchases in GrandRich Master;
  • Deliver rewarded video advertisements and grant in-game bonuses when you choose to watch them;
  • Respond to support requests;
  • Comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive the advertising data Google collects. We do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children.

4. Sharing of information

We may share limited information related to GrandRich Master with:

  • Google AdMob — to serve rewarded video ads and honor user consent;
  • Apple — App Store purchases and related services;
  • Google — Google Play Billing on Android;
  • Hosting providers — to serve this website;
  • Authorities — when required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local GrandRich Master game data remains on your device until you use Reset game in your Profile, delete the App, or clear its data. Purchase records are retained by Apple or Google according to their policies. Support emails are retained as needed to resolve your request and for legitimate business or legal purposes, then deleted when no longer needed. You may ask us to delete your support emails at any time.

6. Children

GrandRich Master is intended for an adult audience (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App asks players to confirm their age before playing. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for this website and rely on Apple’s and Google’s secure purchase and account systems for store transactions related to GrandRich Master. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe there has been unauthorized access related to the App.

8. Your choices

  • You may erase all GrandRich Master data with Reset game in your Profile or by uninstalling the App.
  • You may manage or request refunds for store purchases through Apple or Google.
  • On iOS you can allow or stop tracking at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you can keep playing without watching any.
  • You may contact us about GrandRich Master privacy or support emails: GrandRichMaster@baihatlabs.com.

9. International processing

Your device stores local GrandRich Master game data. Apple and Google may process account, payment and advertising data in multiple countries. This website is hosted on servers that may be outside your country of residence.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of GrandRich Master after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
